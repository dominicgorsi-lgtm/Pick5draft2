Authentication tells us who sent a request. Authorization and business rules decide what that identity may do. A disabled button or a hidden field does not enforce either boundary. All six flaws require a signed-in player account.

Open the console and use Compare as Alice for each scene. Start with an easy privacy failure and build toward control of the pool. On this hosted website, the first pane is an illustrative vulnerable outcome and the second is the actual guarded response. The local lab can execute the unsafe cases. Leave role escalation last because it changes Alice's role. The hosted site renders the XSS example as text; it does not execute it.
05 / FIX FIRST

Control of the pool.
Integrity of the pot.

1 / ROLE MASS ASSIGNMENT

Player → commissioner

One authenticated request.
No victim interaction.
Broad administrative scope.

2 / PAYOUT TAMPERING

$0 earned → $999.99 recorded

Direct financial-record corruption.
No winning card required.
Narrower than full commissioner access.

Both receive provisional P0/Critical business priority. This is a remediation order, not a formal CVSS rating. An automatically trusted payment integration would make payout tampering an immediate payment incident. No transfer occurs in our demo.

If completed-game outcomes can be used before score sync, move the deadline bypass ahead of sealed-pick disclosure. Explain why a severity label alone does not choose the next engineering task.
07 / THE REPAIR

Put the decision
back on the server.

Protect the boundaries

Allowlist profile fields.
Bind SQL parameters.
Enforce ownership and deadlines.
Render player names as text.

Protect the outcome

Calculate awards from verified results.
Settle atomically and once.
Audit privilege and payout changes.
Test invariants, including negative cases.

The hosted routes enforce the safeguards. The separate local lab contains the original vulnerable routes. To verify a repair, rerun the same malicious requests against the fixed route and confirm normal behavior still works. Include checks for both confidentiality and financial conservation.
08 / MY VERIFICATION BAR

A fix is a behavior
you can prove.

  1. A player cannot gain commissioner rights.
  2. Sealed entries stay sealed on every read path.
  3. A closed week rejects direct API writes.
  4. Search input cannot alter query structure.
  5. Player names render as inert text.
  6. Awards equal the pot or the pot carries forward.
Explain the regression strategy: assert denial and confirm the protected record did not change. Verify the $500 fixture has exactly two $250 awards, and a no-winner week carries its full amount. Current automated tests verify the lab and its comparisons; this slide is the acceptance bar for a fully repaired product.
